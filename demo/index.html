Who knows the position terms

PartyKnowsWhy
Public observerAccounts, the 600 mUSDC escrow and 601 mUSDC maker reserve, salted commitment and quote digests, two 144-byte ciphertexts, oracle rounds, settlement amounts, withdrawalsEverything on chain. Size, entry, and margin stay hidden until settlement amounts constrain them.
MakerExact size and entry, plus an independent quote blinderThe maker receives the quote terms off chain, recomputes the quote digest, and signs an approval bound to the staged opening mark. It must know what it is pricing. It does not learn margin.
Keeper A and Keeper BFull opening: size, entry, margin, position blinderEach decrypts its own on-chain envelope and checks the commitment before signing admission, so either one can prove liquidation alone. That is what lets Keeper B act while the trader and Keeper A are offline.
TraderFull opening and the position blinderNeeded to prove a voluntary close.
